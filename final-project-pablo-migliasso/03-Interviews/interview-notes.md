# Consolidated interview notes

## Coverage

The supplied dossier contains 10 interview records: 2 detailed operator interviews and 8 condensed stakeholder interviews. These notes separate reported statements from observations made during shadowing.

## Prior-authorization coordinator

- Workflow: receives work from Caregate queues, determines payer requirements, submits through payer portals or phone channels, records local status, chases pending cases, and communicates with clinics.
- Reported volume: 2,400 requests/month across 14 coordinators.
- Reported exception rates: 61% require a chase; 18% initially denied.
- Tools: Caregate, payer portals, phone, email, and a personal 431-row/14-column tracker containing about 12 years of notes.
- Pain: repeated status checks, inconsistent attachments, payer differences, clinic scheduling uncertainty, and dependence on coordinator memory.
- Direct signal: the personal tracker is both critical operating context and an unmanaged single-person dependency.

## Referral-intake coordinator

- Workflow: opens incoming faxes, interprets documents, looks up providers/routing, enters data in Caregate, seeks missing information, and routes to clinics.
- Reported volume: 5,800 referrals/month; 62% fax; 9 coordinators.
- Tools: RightFax/eFax, Caregate, Microsoft 365, and an approximately 900-row provider lookup spreadsheet.
- Reported exception rate: 25% problematic; the 10-case observed sample showed 20%, which is too small to treat as a stable estimate.
- Pain: missing insurance or provider details, ambiguous destinations, duplicate interpretation, and back-and-forth with senders.

## Revenue Cycle and appeals stakeholders

- Reported volume: 47,000 claims/month and a 12.3% first-pass denial rate.
- Measured work queue: 3,100 denials/month; 34% attributed to missing/invalid authorization; 58% overturned on appeal.
- Tools: Caregate, ClearBridge, payer portals, SharePoint, and approximately 1,400 unindexed appeal documents.
- Contradiction: reported staff throughput of 15–20 denials/day across 11 staff implies 3,575–4,767 monthly cases, inconsistent with the measured 3,100 total.

## Credentialing stakeholder

- Reported volume: 35 new enrollments/quarter and 60 recredentialing cycles/quarter across about 14 payers.
- Reported handling: about 40 minutes per payer form when information is complete.
- Pain: status chasing, missing documents, payer-specific requirements, and weak shared visibility.
- Evidence limit: no shadowing was completed; time and exception estimates remain interview-only.

## Patient Growth stakeholder

- Reported workload: 3 people spend 6 hours each assembling the monthly report, plus 3 hours on narrative/deck work, or 21 team-hours/month.
- Tools: HubSpot, spreadsheets, and presentation tools.
- Opportunity: deterministic data consolidation is likely more important than generative narrative creation.

## Governance and technology stakeholders

- No organization-wide AI policy was reported.
- PHI-bearing AI requires a BAA and risk assessment with a reported minimum lead time of 6–8 weeks.
- Caregate read access requires about 2 weeks; write access requires vendor approval estimated at 8–12 weeks.
- RightFax API access is reportedly available in about 1 day.
- Most payer portals do not expose APIs; only 3 payers may be reachable through an unpurchased clearinghouse product.
