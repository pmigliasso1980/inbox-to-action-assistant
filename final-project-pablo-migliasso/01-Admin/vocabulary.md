# Healthcare revenue-cycle vocabulary

| Term | Plain definition | Why it matters here |
|---|---|---|
| Cost to collect | Administrative cost required to collect patient revenue, usually as a share of revenue | The board and sponsor use it as the primary margin signal. |
| Days in accounts receivable | Average time between billing and cash collection | It captures elapsed-time harm that staff-hour estimates miss. |
| First-pass claim acceptance | Share of claims accepted on first submission | A decline indicates upstream quality or authorization failures. |
| Prior authorization | Payer approval required before specified care | Delays block care and missing authorizations drive denials. |
| Payer | Insurer responsible for adjudicating a claim | Seven payer workflows create different rules, portals, and exceptions. |
| CPT code | Standard code identifying a medical procedure | Payers use procedure codes to determine authorization and payment rules. |
| NPI | National Provider Identifier for a clinician or organization | Staff repeatedly key it into referrals, payer calls, and enrollment forms. |
| Denial | Payer refusal of a submitted claim | Denials create rework, write-offs, and longer accounts-receivable time. |
| Appeal | Request asking a payer to reverse a denial | Staff assemble evidence and precedent under long response times. |
| Clearinghouse | Intermediary that validates and exchanges claims with payers | ClearBridge holds structured denial data and supports an API. |
| Credentialing | Verification of a clinician’s qualifications | It precedes payer enrollment and involves sensitive documents. |
| Payer enrollment | Registration that lets Kestrel bill a payer for a clinician | Late enrollment makes delivered care temporarily unbillable. |
| Protected health information (PHI) | Identifiable health information protected by HIPAA | It determines tool, vendor, logging, and access controls. |
| Business Associate Agreement (BAA) | Contract requiring a vendor to protect PHI | Kestrel cannot use a general AI vendor with PHI until one exists. |

Source: dossier B1, B3, B6, and B9; definitions are plain-language interpretations for this engagement.
