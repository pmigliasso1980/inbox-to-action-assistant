# Executive readout outline and timed speaker notes

Target runtime: **22 minutes (proposed)**. The slide deck is an executive decision artifact, not a narration of every file.

## Slide 1 — Administrative burden is concentrated in three broken handoffs (1:30)

State the decision: approve a referral-intake assistant pilot and a denial-data prerequisite. Emphasize that the recommendation is augmentation with human accountability, not autonomy.

## Slide 2 — The evidence is strong enough for two pilots and weak in two named areas (2:30)

Cover 10 interviews, 2 shadowing sessions/255 minutes, 153 survey responses/32%, 13 inventoried workflows, and 3 detailed maps. Name the limitations: no Revenue Cycle shadowing, no credentialing shadowing, 20% Practice Operations survey response, and no central-scheduling coverage.

## Slide 3 — Staff bridge fragmented systems with spreadsheets, inboxes, phone calls, and memory (2:30)

Walk through the common operating pattern across prior authorization, referral intake, and denials. Explain that wait states and local workarounds are design signals, not employee failures.

## Slide 4 — Referral intake is the best bounded AI pilot (2:30)

Show 3,596 fax referrals/month projected, 6.3–8.1 minutes/fax projected from ten observed cases, and 377.6–485.5 hours/month projected. Clarify that this is addressable workload, not promised savings.

## Slide 5 — Denial leakage cannot be improved until the numbers reconcile (2:30)

Show 5,781 projected first-pass denials/month, 3,100 measured worked denials/month, and 2,681 projected difference. Explain why a deterministic feedback dashboard precedes generative summaries.

## Slide 6 — Kestrel is experimenting faster than it is governing (2:30)

Present 2.0/5.0 assessed maturity. Contrast 51% work AI use and 71% personal-account use with 6 formally trained respondents, no AI policy, and a 6–8 week PHI review.

## Slide 7 — The portfolio favors augmentation and prerequisites over autonomy (2:30)

Place six opportunities on the impact/feasibility matrix. Call out referral intake, denial feedback, and the patient-balance autonomous agent as the explicit no-go.

## Slide 8 — Two staged pilots make failure visible and reversible (3:00)

Explain read-only operation, synthetic/de-identified data first, mandatory human verification, frozen golden set, production sampling, and the kill thresholds. State that no Caregate write access is needed for the first gate.

## Slide 9 — Three named decisions unlock the next six to eight weeks (2:00)

Ask executives to approve the sequence, name Practice Operations and Revenue Cycle owners by 2026-10-16, and approve or restrict the PHI data path by 2026-12-11. End with the agreed decision date, not a generic thank-you.
