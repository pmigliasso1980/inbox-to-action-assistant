# AI use disclosure

AI was used to structure the supplied fictional dossier, draft artifacts, check arithmetic, generate diagram and presentation code, and create the deterministic evaluation harness. All factual claims were constrained to the supplied Kestrel evidence; computed values are labeled projected, maturity judgments are labeled assessed, and future thresholds are labeled proposed. No real patient data or external confidential information was used.

Human responsibility remains with the candidate. Before submission, the candidate should review every artifact against the source dossier, record the executive readout personally, enter actual time spent, and decide whether the recommendations represent their judgment.
