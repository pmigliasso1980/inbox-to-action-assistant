# Prediction check

The week-zero prediction expected fragmented systems, spreadsheet workarounds, status chasing, uneven AI use, and governance constraints. The evidence confirmed all five, but the strongest surprise was how much the data-definition problem matters: denial volumes conflict before any model is introduced.

The initial instinct was to prioritize prior authorization because its apparent workload is largest. Discovery changed that recommendation. Portal and phone dependence, unknown authorization-document rates, and Caregate approval timelines make prior authorization a strategic opportunity but a weak first pilot. Referral intake is smaller but more testable, safer, and more reversible.

The prediction also underestimated organizational appetite: 41 survey respondents volunteered for pilots. That improves change feasibility but does not remove the need for policy, training, and ownership.
