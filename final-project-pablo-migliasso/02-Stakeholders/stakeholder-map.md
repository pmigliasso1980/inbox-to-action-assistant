# Stakeholder and influence map

| Role | Formal remit | Influence and dependency | Engagement posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| CEO | Clinical and corporate leadership | Connects board pressure to physician burden; executive air cover | Keep informed through patient-access consequences, not tool language. |
| COO, sponsor | Administrative performance and budget | Primary sponsor; budget available; no named AI owner | Direct, metric-led; likely decision maker and escalation route. |
| Chief Compliance Officer | Privacy, vendor risk, regulatory control | Can block or enable every PHI-bearing pilot | Engage before tool choice; likely design partner rather than late approver. |
| Director of IT | Access, security review, integrations | Owns service accounts and API timelines; knows hidden systems | Technical feasibility owner and potential platform champion. |
| VP Revenue Cycle | Cost to collect and accounts receivable | Owns denial teams and performance narrative | Strong operational owner candidate for denial work. |
| Director of Practice Operations | Referral, authorization, scheduling, records | Controls the largest in-scope department and frontline access | Candidate owner for referral-intake pilot. |
| Director of People Operations | Recruiting and credentialing | Identified sensitive-data risks without prompting | Valuable governance ally; not yet deep in credentialing operations. |
| Marketing Manager | Patient Growth reporting | One of few sanctioned AI users; can model safe adoption | Champion for low-risk self-service use cases. |
| Prior authorization coordinator | Operational knowledge and tracker ownership | Informal single point of failure; colleagues depend on her rules | Champion only if design preserves clinical judgment and enables leave. |
| Referral intake coordinator | Fax intake and keying | Owns a **900-row reported** lookup spreadsheet; understands exceptions | Strong pilot user and validation reviewer. |
| Billing specialist | Denials and appeals | Sees upstream defects and unsafe personal-AI failure firsthand | Champion for retrieval, skeptical of autonomous drafting. |
| Credentialing lead | Enrollment and recredentialing | Knows payer-specific work and financial consequences | Owner candidate after workflow observation. |
| Central Scheduling lead | Scheduling for **70 people (reported)** | Important downstream consumer of authorization status | **Gap:** not identified or interviewed; must be added before build approval. |
| Harborline board partners | Investment oversight | Reinforce cost pressure but do not own operations | Readout audience; no day-to-day pilot role. |

Sources: B1.3–B1.6, B3, B6, B8, B10.
