# Ranked opportunity backlog

## Scoring method

Each factor uses a five-point scale. **Priority index = (2 × impact) + feasibility − effort − change
difficulty.** Impact receives double weight because the engagement exists to improve business outcomes;
feasibility, effort, and adoption friction prevent attractive but undeliverable ideas from ranking first.
Ties break on impact, then evidence quality.

| Rank | Opportunity | Impact | Feasibility | Effort | Change difficulty | Index | Quadrant | Verdict |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 1 | OPP-01 Referral intake extraction assistant | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 9 | Do first | AUGMENT |
| 2 | OPP-02 Denial root-cause feedback dashboard | 4 | 5 | 2 | 2 | 9 | Do first, non-AI | GO |
| 3 | OPP-03 Appeal precedent finder | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 4 | Strategic | AUGMENT |
| 4 | OPP-04 Prior authorization chase digest | 5 | 2 | 5 | 3 | 4 | Strategic | AUGMENT |
| 5 | OPP-05 Credentialing and enrollment worklist | 4 | 2 | 4 | 2 | 4 | Process first | GO, defer AI |
| 6 | OPP-06 Autonomous patient-balance agent | 2 | 1 | 5 | 5 | -5 | Deprioritize | NO GO |

OPP-01 wins the tie because it touches **377.6–485.5 hours/month projected** of observed work and has a
one-day reported RightFax access path, while preserving human judgment. OPP-02 should begin in parallel as
a low-effort non-AI prerequisite.

The matrix is in [`matrix.png`](matrix.png). Axis x measures delivery difficulty as effort + change
difficulty + (6 − feasibility), giving a range of 3–15. Axis y is impact, 1–5.
