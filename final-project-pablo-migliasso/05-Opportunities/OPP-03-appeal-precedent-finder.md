# OPP-03: Appeal precedent finder

**Department:** Revenue Cycle
**Source:** WF-R02
**Primitives:** research, retrieval
**Verdict:** **AUGMENT — stage after governance approval**

## Workflow change

Index the **1,400 appeal documents reported** in SharePoint inside an approved PHI environment. For a
denial, retrieve the closest previously successful examples and show source documents. The specialist
selects evidence, verifies policy currency, and authors the final appeal; the system does not fabricate or
submit a letter.

## Impact

A standard appeal consumes **40 minutes reported**, but monthly appeal count is unknown. No savings figure
is defensible until the team records appeal volume and retrieval time. Value also comes from reducing the
risk that staff reuse stale precedent or unsafe public-model text.

| Check | Result | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Rule-based | Partial | Retrieval criteria are testable; legal/clinical relevance remains judgment. |
| Digital/structured input | Partial | Documents are digital but lack naming convention, index, or owner. |
| Volume/frequency | Unknown | Denials are high volume; appeal subset is not supplied. |
| Stable process | Yes | Staff already search precedent before drafting. |
| Data accessible | Conditional | SharePoint is accessible, but documents contain PHI and require approved controls. |

**Impact 3/5; feasibility 3/5; effort 3/5; change difficulty 2/5.** Suggested owner: VP Revenue Cycle.
